Privacy

How a recruitment site like this one handles personal information, and what this concept site does with yours: nothing.

This is a concept site

Tierney is a fictional company. This website is a design demonstration by Ufuk Karakuş and is not a real business. The forms on it do not send anything anywhere: there is no server behind them, no account exists, and nothing you type is collected, stored or read by anyone.

The only thing the site keeps is the choices you make under “For you” on the Find a job page: the documents you hold, what you have done and what you want next. They are saved in your own browser’s local storage so that the job pages can show whether you can apply, and they never leave your device. You can remove them at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

What a recruitment site like this would collect

If Tierney were a real agency, applying for a job or sending a CV would involve the following personal information:

  • your name and phone number, so that a consultant can call you back;
  • the licences, cards and certificates you hold, such as a Category C or CE licence, a Driver CPC or a tachograph card, so that you are only put forward for work you are qualified to do;
  • whether you have the right to work in Ireland, which an agency must confirm before placing you;
  • your CV, if you choose to send one;
  • for employers, a company name, a contact name and a phone number.

Why it would be collected

Only to match you with suitable work, to check your documents with your consent before an employer sees your profile, to keep an eye on expiry dates such as your Driver CPC, and to contact you about applications and interviews. It would never be sold or used for advertising.

How long it would be kept

For as long as you are looking for work through the agency, and for up to twelve months after your last contact with it, after which it would be deleted. Records of a placement would be kept for as long as employment and tax law requires.

How to ask for it to be deleted

You would email hello@tierney.example or ring 01 000 6622 and ask. Your details would be removed within one month, and you would be told when it was done. Because this is a concept site, there is nothing to delete: no information about you is held.
